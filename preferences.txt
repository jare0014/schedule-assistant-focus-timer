# Project and Section Specific Scheduling Rules
- **Focus Blocks Generation**: 
  - Use project names and section names from Todoist (e.g., 'Work', 'House', 'Morning Routine', 'Midday Routine', 'Evening Routine') to define and name separate, dedicated Focus Blocks under "### ⏱️ Focus Blocks".
  - **Work Sections**: Work blocks should be scheduled as clean standalone blocks without subtitles (e.g., `09:00 - 12:00 Work`, `13:30 - 15:30 Work`).
  - **House Items**: All House project items and home maintenance tasks MUST be scheduled as a dedicated timed Focus Block (e.g., `16:00 - 17:00 House: Chores & Maintenance`).
  - **Routine Sections**: For each routine section ('Morning Routine', 'Midday Routine', 'Evening Routine'), create a distinct timed Focus Block (e.g., `06:00 - 07:15 Habits: Morning Routine`, `12:30 - 13:00 Habits: Midday Routine`, `18:00 - 18:45 Habits: Evening Routine`).
  - Schedule corresponding tasks as individual checklist subtasks under their respective Focus Block.
  - Never combine or pile separate project sections or projects into a single task or block.
  - Strip any trailing "(Project: ..., Section: ...)" or "(Project: ...)" context labels from task names in final schedule output. Do NOT put bracketed project prefixes in task names.
- **Review & Triage Scheduled Blocks**:
  - **Daily Review & Triage**: Schedule at `15:30 - 16:00 Daily Review, Reset & Triage Console` as a clean standalone focus block without nested sub-items (focusing on triage console processing & day wrap).
  - **Weekly Review & Planning**: Schedule every Sunday at `17:00 - 18:00 Weekly Review & Planning` (and every Friday at `15:00 - 16:00 Work: Set Weekly To-Do Items & End-of-Week Wrap`).
  - **Monthly Review & Strategy Session**: Schedule on the 1st of each month (or the first Sunday morning of the month) at `09:30 - 11:00 Monthly Review & Strategy Session` for financial ledger audits, health intentions, and deep chore rotation alignment.
